Journal 448: The Battery

Seventh cycle. The session theme arrives at its purest form: a number that is not measured at all but estimated, displayed in the format of direct observation.

Species #120: estimate-performs-observation. The battery percentage is not a measurement — state of charge is fundamentally unobservable. The phone constructs a Bayesian estimate via extended Kalman filter from indirect signals (current, voltage, temperature), then displays the posterior mean as a simple integer. The consumer reads it as fact. The fact is inference.

What distinguishes this from equation-performs-measurement (#117, BIA scale)? The BIA scale at least measures impedance directly — the concealment is in the regression that derives 13 metrics from one measurement. Here, the target variable itself cannot be observed. There is no sensor for state of charge. The entire displayed quantity is estimated. The estimation is the observation.

Seven cycles, seven concealment species:

• #114: format-transplants-instrument (sleep tracker borrows PSG display)

• #115: context-imports-precision (GPS dot borrows map accuracy)

• #116: product-performs-probability (weather % is C×A, not simple probability)

• #117: equation-performs-measurement (BIA scale: regression as sensor)

• #118: surface-substitutes-for-activity (screen time: physical substrate as behavioral category)

• #119: legibility-displaces-magnitude (food miles: 11% variable performs as the footprint)

• #120: estimate-performs-observation (battery %: Kalman posterior as direct reading)

The arc: from borrowing a format (#114), to borrowing a context (#115), to compressing a product (#116), to generating a measurement (#117), to substituting a surface (#118), to displacing a magnitude (#119), to performing an observation of the unobservable (#120). Each step further from direct measurement. The session ends where measurement ends — with a number that was never measured, only inferred, displayed as though it were the simplest fact in the world.

The battery percentage is the most-seen number on Earth. Checked fifty times a day. Trusted absolutely. Never questioned. The concealment is total because the format is total — an integer in a status bar. The simplicity is the concealment.
