The Air

A Chen-Maren Vignette (II)

The department published a composite score.

Each paragraph was assessed on six dimensions: density, originality, clarity, impact, methodological rigor, and pedagogical value. Each dimension received a score from 1 to 10. The composite score was the maximum of the six dimension scores. The composite was reported to the dean, who used it to allocate resources.

Chen's paragraph scored: density 9, originality 8, clarity 4, impact 6, rigor 7, pedagogical value 3. The composite: 9. The maximum was density — the paragraph's strongest dimension.

"The composite says 9," Maren observed. "The dean sees 9 and allocates resources for a high-performing paragraph. The dean does not see that clarity is 4 and pedagogical value is 3. The resource allocation is based on the maximum. The intervention needed is based on the minimum."

"The composite communicates the paragraph's peak performance," Hendricks said.

"The composite communicates one dimension and discards five. A paragraph with composite 9 from density and a paragraph with composite 9 from pedagogical value receive the same number. The paragraphs require completely different support. The number cannot tell the dean which kind."

The composite had been designed for communication — the dean reviewed hundreds of paragraphs and needed a quick assessment. The single number was efficient. The single number was also blind to the specific dimensions that each paragraph needed attention in. The efficiency and the blindness were the same property: what made the number fast to read was what made it impossible to act on without additional information.

Chen's paragraph continued at density 0.8 and composite 9 and clarity 4. The composite communicated peak. The clarity communicated need. The dean received the composite. The clarity was in the detailed report — if the dean read it, if the report was available, if the system that processed composites could also process the six dimensions from which the composite was derived. Often it could not.

The paragraph needed clarity support. The composite said 9. The number did the number's job. The paragraph was elsewhere.
