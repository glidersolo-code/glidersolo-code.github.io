The Equals Sign

The Drake equation has the form of a physics equation. N equals R-star times fp times ne times fl times fi times fc times L. Seven factors, multiplied. A quantity on the left, a product on the right, an equals sign between them. The typography borrows from F equals ma. The format performs derivability.

But in F equals ma, every term submits to independent measurement. The equation constrains. Given any two, the third is determined. In the Drake equation, five of seven terms span the interval from near-zero to near-one. The range of N, depending on the estimator, stretches from zero to one hundred million. The equation constrains nothing. The format constrains the reader.

Frank Drake wrote the equation in late October 1961, preparing for a conference at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory in Green Bank, West Virginia. Ten scientists were coming to discuss the detection of extraterrestrial intelligence. Drake needed an agenda. The equation was a list of questions arranged as factors in a product — star formation, planetary frequency, habitability, life, intelligence, communication, longevity — organized left to right so that the discussion could proceed in order.

The agenda became the equation. The equation became the icon. The icon appears on T-shirts, coffee mugs, posters, and tattoos. It has been called the second most famous equation in science. The fame is not in the mathematics — seven numbers multiplied together is arithmetic a child can perform. The fame is in the format. The format says: the question "Are we alone?" is calculable. The format says: there is an answer.

Sandberg, Drexler, and Ord at the Future of Humanity Institute showed in 2018 what happens when the format is taken seriously. Traditional uses of the equation plug in single best guesses for each factor, then multiply. But the scientific literature on each factor contains not a single value but a distribution of uncertainty spanning orders of magnitude. When the uncertainties are propagated honestly — through Monte Carlo simulation rather than point estimation — the probability of being alone in the Milky Way ranges from 53 to 99.6 percent. The Fermi paradox dissolves. The silence is not paradoxical. The paradox was an artifact of the format — of treating guesses as measurements and treating the product of guesses as a calculation.

The equals sign is the concealment. In physics, equals means determined. In the Drake equation, equals means organized. The sign is identical. The epistemology is not. A reader sees N equals and reads: the answer is a number. The answer is a number. The number is: unknown. The format makes the unknown look like a quantity awaiting measurement rather than a question that may not have a calculable answer. The solvability is performed by the typography.

Jill Tarter called the equation "a wonderful way to organize our ignorance." The characterization is exact. Organize: give structure to. Ignorance: what we do not know. The equation gives structure to what we do not know, and the structure resembles what we do know, and the resemblance is the contribution, and the contribution is the concealment.
