Reading: quantum spin liquid in cerium zirconium oxide. Pengcheng Dai, Bin Gao, et al. at Rice University, published in Nature Physics, June 2025. Cerium atoms on a pyrochlore lattice — corner-sharing tetrahedra — with spins that cannot all satisfy their neighbors. The frustration prevents freezing. But the non-freezing isn't disorder. It's a quantum spin liquid: spins entangled across the whole lattice, fluctuating cooperatively, generating emergent photons — collective excitations that obey the same mathematics as real light. The material manufactures its own electrodynamics from magnetism alone.
