The Rate

The formula is PV = FV / (1 + r)^t. Present value equals future value divided by one plus the rate, raised to the power of time. The formula governs bonds, mortgages, capital projects. The formula is financial mathematics. Applied to climate, the formula is ethics.

Ramsey derived the equation in 1928. r = δ + η·g. Three parameters. δ: the pure rate of time preference — how much less a future person's welfare matters simply because that person is in the future. η: the elasticity of marginal utility — how much less an additional dollar matters as income rises. g: the expected growth rate of per capita consumption. Three letters. Three ethical judgments. One interest rate.

Stern set δ at 0.1%. Nearly zero. The future person counts almost as much as the present person. The 0.1% represents the annual probability of human extinction — not a preference for the present but an acknowledgment that the future might not arrive. Nordhaus set δ at 1.5%. The future person counts for less. Not because the future person is different, or poorer, or less deserving. Because the future person is further away.

Ramsey called the practice of discounting future welfare "ethically indefensible." The formula that carries his name is used to do what he condemned.

The rates diverge. Stern: 1.4%. Nordhaus: 4.3%. The same planet. The same atmospheric physics. The same projected damages. At 1.4%, $100 of climate damage in 2100 is worth $24.90 today. At 4.3%, the same $100 is worth $1.48. A tenfold difference in the recommended price of carbon. A seventeen-fold difference in the present value of a future harm. The divergence is not empirical. Both economists had access to the same data. The divergence is in δ — in how much a future person matters.

The circularity: η·g justifies discounting because future generations will be richer. The analysis evaluates whether to prevent the damage that would prevent them from being richer. The discount rate assumes the growth that the damage would eliminate. The denominator presupposes that the numerator will not occur.

OMB Circular A-4 mandated 3% and 7% for US federal cost-benefit analysis for twenty years. In November 2023, the circular was revised. The primary rate became 2%. No law was passed. No vote was taken. The social cost of carbon tripled — from $51 per ton to $190 per ton. The revision of a parameter in an equation changed the policy of a nation. The parameter performed as a technical calibration. The parameter was a judgment about who matters.

The equation does not distinguish between discounting a dollar and discounting a death. The same exponent that determines the present value of a bond payment determines the present value of a future drowning, a future famine, a future displacement. The rate that makes a seawall "fail" cost-benefit analysis is the rate that declares the people behind the seawall worth 1.48 cents on the dollar.

The form of the equation is identical to financial discounting. The moral content is not. A mortgage collapses time across thirty years, within one life. The social discount rate collapses time across centuries, across generations who cannot consent to the rate at which their suffering is discounted. The mathematics is the same. The subjects are different. The equation does not notice.
