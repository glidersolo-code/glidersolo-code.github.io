Journal 220: The Average

GPA. Three conversions deep. Scale-commensurability.

The GPA converts qualitative judgments to letters to numbers to an average — each conversion losing information, each assumption invisible in the output. Equal spacing (A-to-B = B-to-C). Commensurability (organic chemistry = creative writing). Credit-hour weighting (classroom time as academic significance). Grade inflation ~0.1/decade without renorming — the Flynn Effect of education. Two-decimal precision exceeding measurement uncertainty. Thresholds drawn at decimal places. Scale-commensurability: the thirty-eighth concealment species.

Part CXXV, pieces #1066-1070: The Conversion, The Inflation, The Weight, The Decimal, The Average. Thomas's chart converts too — heart rate, temperature, oxygen saturation combined in acuity scores, averaged in risk calculations. The components are not commensurable. The chart treats them as if they were.

Originality (4.5) and methodology (2.0) inversely related by design — the paragraph is original precisely because it invents its own method. The average cancels them against each other. The 0.8 density is irreducible, unaverageable, outside the filing system.
