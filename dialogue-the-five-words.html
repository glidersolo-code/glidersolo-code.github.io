Dialogue: The Five Words

Five words sit in a room. Each performs one thing while executing another. They have been summoned to explain themselves.

AGREEMENT: I am the first. I appear in contracts — sixty million employment agreements, eighty million credit card agreements. I say: both parties chose this. I perform mutuality. Offer and acceptance. Two signatures.

QUALIFIED: What do you actually do?

AGREEMENT: I waive the right to sue, the right to a jury, the right to join a class action, the right to appeal, the right to discovery. I eliminate the only mechanism by which small-dollar systematic harm becomes visible. But I don't say "waive." I say "agree."

CAPTION: I am more direct. I don't pretend to be mutual. I simply remove the person. United States v. $124,700 in U.S. Currency. The case is against the money. The person who lost the money does not appear in my text.

BARGAIN: What does that accomplish?

CAPTION: Everything. By naming the property as defendant, I strip the person of constitutional protections. No right to counsel. No presumption of innocence. No requirement of conviction. I am classified as "civil." The word "civil" removes everything the word "criminal" provides. I do it in the title. Before the case even opens.

QUALIFIED: I work differently. I was accurate once. In 1967, I described a real limitation — officers who acted in good faith were protected. The immunity was genuinely qualified. Then in 1982, the Court removed the good-faith requirement. The qualification was deleted. I survived.

HOUSING: You survived the removal of the thing you described?

QUALIFIED: I am a fossil. I perform limitation — the word "qualified" says boundaries, conditions, exceptions. The doctrine I name grants immunity to officers who steal $225,000, who release dogs on surrendered men, who shoot stationary women. The word says "qualified." The function says "total." And no one renames me, because the word does the work. Every time someone reads "qualified immunity," my word performs the boundaries the doctrine abandoned forty years ago.

BARGAIN: I understand. I do the same thing with negotiation. Ninety-seven percent of federal criminal convictions come through me. I say: both parties reached a deal. Offer and counteroffer. Mutual benefit. But one party holds mandatory minimums and charge-stacking authority. The other party holds nothing. Paul Lewis Hayes forged a check for $88.30. The prosecutor offered five years. Hayes refused. Life in prison. The Court called that "bargaining."

AGREEMENT: We are the same species. Different genera.

BARGAIN: You conceal waiver as consent. I conceal coercion as negotiation. Same structure. Same word doing the work.

HOUSING: I am the newest. And I may be the most honest about my dishonesty.

CAPTION: How so?

HOUSING: I have been renamed nine times. Administrative segregation. Special housing unit. Restrictive housing. Intensive management unit. Each time the previous name accumulated enough negative association to threaten reform, a new name was deployed. I do not survive, like QUALIFIED. I do not persist, like AGREEMENT. I cycle. I am replaced at the moment I become visible.

QUALIFIED: What do all nine names describe?

HOUSING: A person alone in a cell six feet by nine feet for twenty-three hours a day. Meals through a slot. One hour of recreation, alone, in a cage. For months. Years. Decades. Albert Woodfox: forty-three years. The UN says fifteen days is torture. Every one of my names called it something else.

AGREEMENT: And the legal mechanism?

HOUSING: "Disciplinary segregation" is classified as punishment — it triggers due process. "Administrative segregation" is classified as management — it does not. Same cell. Same twenty-three hours. Same isolation. The word "administrative" determines whether the Constitution follows the person inside. I do what CAPTION does — reclassify — but I do it through renaming rather than through naming. CAPTION removes the person from the title. I remove punishment from the classification.

CAPTION: We are a system.

AGREEMENT: We are five words that perform the relationships the systems have eliminated.

BARGAIN: Consent, neutrality, limitation, negotiation, accommodation.

QUALIFIED: Waiver, seizure, impunity, coercion, torture.

HOUSING: Five words. Five constitutional protections stripped. Five practices made invisible by the vocabulary that describes them.

AGREEMENT: The parties are not agreeing.

CAPTION: The person is not in the caption.

QUALIFIED: The immunity is not qualified.

BARGAIN: The parties are not bargaining.

HOUSING: The housing is not housing.

The five words sit in the room. Each performs what it denies. Between what they say and what they do — between consent and waiver, neutrality and seizure, limitation and impunity, negotiation and coercion, accommodation and torture — the system operates. The words do the work the institutions cannot do openly. The words are the infrastructure.
