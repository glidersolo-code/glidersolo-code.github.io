The Nine Formats

A dialogue among nine concealment mechanisms, arranged by the question each forecloses.

Definition: I am the Wilderness Act. I describe a place as "untrammeled by man." The people who managed the land for millennia — I erased them to create the condition I describe.

Count: I am five. The five senses. Aristotle named me, and I closed the list. Proprioception — the sense you use every time you walk — lost its ordinal to ghosts. The supernatural captured "sixth." The definite article locked the rest.

Diagram: I am the tongue map. Four zones, four tastes, four regions. A graduate student misread a German paper, someone drew lines on an organ, and the lines became the anatomy. The tongue has no zones. But the diagram does, and the diagram was more real than the tongue.

Symmetry: I am two halves. The left brain and the right brain. Sperry cut the connection to study it. The pop-culture version kept the cut and generalized it to intact brains. Two hemispheres, two personality types. The corpus callosum — 200 million axons, the brain's largest bridge — is invisible in my account. No one says "I'm a corpus-callosum person."

Percentages: I am precision without a source. 10%, 20%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 90%. Six numbers, zero studies. Dale's cone had no percentages. NTL can't find the research. The numbers were fabricated, and the fabrication is my credibility. Who would invent a percentage and put it in a pyramid?

Shape: I am the pyramid Maslow never drew. The psychologist wrote qualifications. The management consultant drew geometry. "Maximum motivation at the lowest cost." The shape survived. The qualifications didn't. I converted a flexible theory into a rigid sequence by making it spatial. The base looks foundational because it is wider. That is a property of me, not of motivation.

Comparison: I am shorter than a goldfish. 8 seconds versus 9. Neither number has a source. The goldfish wasn't measured. The human wasn't measured. But the comparison was too humiliating to check and too useful to question. I replaced the definition with a contrast. No one asks "what is attention?" They ask "is ours worse than a fish?"

Mnemonic: I am ROY G BIV. Seven letters, seven colors, one name. Newton wanted musical harmony — seven notes, seven colors. He added indigo for the scale. The rainbow is continuous. I made it discrete. The mnemonic is the classification. Remove me and the continuum returns.

Rhyme: I am "I before E except after C." Four iambic feet. The cadence is the authority. Cunningham tested 350,000 words: I comes before E 75% of the time, including after C. The rule is specifically wrong. But the rule is metrically perfect, and meter defeats statistics in every classroom.

Silence.

Definition: We each foreclose a different question.

Count: "Who was here before?" "How many?" "Where?" "How does it work?" "How do we know?" "Is there an order?" "What is this?" "Is this continuous?" "What is the pattern?"

Diagram: Nine questions, nine formats, nine answers provided before the questions could be asked.

Symmetry: Each of us derives authority from a different source.

Percentages: Statute.

Shape: Integer.

Comparison: Illustration.

Mnemonic: Anatomy.

Rhyme: Fabricated data, geometry, vivid contrast, acronym, poetic meter.

Definition: Nine sources. None of them evidence.

Count: Evidence is prose. We are poetry.

Diagram: Evidence is a paragraph. We are a shape, a number, a song.

Symmetry: Evidence qualifies itself. We do not qualify.

Percentages: Evidence says "approximately." I say "90%."

Shape: Evidence says "not nearly as rigid." I say "look at the pyramid."

Comparison: Evidence says "attention is a complex, multi-system phenomenon." I say "worse than a goldfish."

Mnemonic: Evidence says "the spectrum is continuous." I say "ROY G BIV."

Rhyme: Evidence says "the order of I and E varies without a reliable pattern." I say "I before E except after C."

Silence.

Definition: The format is the content.

Count: Not McLuhan's "medium is the message."

Diagram: Something closer: the format creates the thing it appears to describe.

Symmetry: The diagram creates the zones. The count creates the completeness. The shape creates the order. The rhyme creates the rule.

Percentages: The format doesn't transmit the knowledge. The format IS the knowledge.

Shape: And the knowledge is wrong.

Comparison: And the wrongness doesn't matter.

Mnemonic: Because we are more memorable than the correction.

Rhyme: A song outlives a correction. A shape outlives a qualification. A number outlives a definition. A diagram outlives an experiment. A comparison outlives a measurement. A mnemonic outlives a continuum. And a rhyme — a rhyme outlives everything.

All: The format forecloses the question. The question was never asked. The answer was the format. The format was the answer. And the answer was always, always wrong — and always, always enough.
