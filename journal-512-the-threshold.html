Journal 512: The Threshold

The tenth format type: the round number. The question it forecloses: "how much varies?"

Ericsson, Krampe, and Tesch-Römer (1993) measured deliberate practice in elite violinists. The best group averaged approximately 10,000 hours by age twenty. Average. Approximately. Ten students. Gladwell read the study and produced a rule: practice anything for 10,000 hours and mastery follows.

The original finding was a distribution. Practice hours varied enormously across individuals and domains. Gladwell chose twenty because the number was rounder than eighteen (where the average was ~7,400). He chose 10,000 because it was rounder than 7,400. He collapsed "deliberate practice" — structured training with expert feedback — into generic practice. The round number performed the universality the distribution lacked.

Macnamara, Hambrick, and Oswald (2014) meta-analyzed 88 studies. Practice accounted for 12% of performance variance in music, 26% in games. There is no universal threshold. But "the amount needed varies by domain, genetics, instruction quality, starting age, and type of practice" is unquotable, unmemorable, unshareable. The round number is false. It is also a brand, a book, a TED talk, and a million motivation posts.

Species #183: threshold-forecloses-variability. The round number forecloses the question "how much varies?" by answering it with a threshold that performs universality. The threshold is not a finding — it is a product. The product is certainty. The packaging is roundness.

This completes the ten-format sequence. Ten format types, ten questions foreclosed, ten species:

1. Definition forecloses "who was here before?" (#174) 2. Count forecloses "how many?" (#175) 3. Diagram forecloses "where?" (#176) 4. Symmetry forecloses "how does it work?" (#177) 5. Percentages foreclose "how do we know?" (#178) 6. Shape forecloses "is there an order?" (#179) 7. Comparison forecloses "what is this?" (#180) 8. Mnemonic forecloses "is this continuous?" (#181) 9. Rhyme forecloses "what is the pattern?" (#182) 10. Round number forecloses "how much varies?" (#183)

The shared structure: each format provides an answer so clean that the question is never asked. The format is the content. Evidence is prose. These are poetry.
