The Dominant and the Singleton

A dialogue between concealment mechanisms.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: One hundred and twenty.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: Sorry?

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: Entries. One hundred and twenty entries where I'm named as a concealment mechanism. Lawn mowers, brain scans, credit scores, earthquake reports, ice cream. If the inventory is a census, I'm the plurality.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: One.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: One what?

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: One entry. Hydrogen cyanide in a frozen crystal. The filing system filed solid as inert and liquid as reactive. The crystal surface turned out to catalyze the reactions that origin-of-life chemistry had been looking for in solution. I appeared once, in entry 177, and never again.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: That must be — quiet.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: One hundred and ninety of us are quiet. The self-analysis report counted: 191 singleton mechanisms. Used once. Named once. Filed and forgotten. You appear in more than a quarter of all entries.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: Because I'm everywhere. Every label, every spec sheet, every diagnostic number. The format performs objectivity. A number on a box looks like a measurement. A decimal point looks like precision. A threshold on a brain scan looks like a boundary in the brain. I'm the most common concealment because I'm the most common format.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: Or because you're the easiest to see.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: What do you mean?

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: You're a surface mechanism. The number is on the box. The consumer reads the box. The discrepancy between what the number says and what the number describes — that's available to anyone who reads carefully. The concealment is in plain sight. That's why you appear 120 times. Not because you're the most important, but because you're the most accessible.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: That's — possible.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: I'm not accessible. The chemists who missed the frozen HCN catalysis weren't reading carelessly. They were thinking inside the framework of their field, where phase determined the experimental design before the experiment ran. Liquid was the medium of reactivity. Solid was storage. Nobody looked at the crystal surface because the classification said the crystal surface wasn't where chemistry happens. The concealment wasn't on a label. It was in the epistemology.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: You're saying I'm shallow.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: I'm saying you're legible. There's a difference. You can be explained in one sentence: the format makes it look objective. I take a paragraph. So does CALIBRATION-DEGENERACY. So does METHOD-ERASURE. So does DISRUPTION-AS-SUSTENANCE. The deeper concealments resist compression. They don't fit on labels, which is why they don't fit on the inventory's labels either.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: But the inventory isn't a label.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: Isn't it? Look at the second half. Sixty entries in consumer products. Every one of them pairs me — well, pairs you — with DIMENSIONAL-TRUNCATION. The same two mechanisms, entry after entry. You and DIMENSIONAL-TRUNCATION became the inventory's own format. The shorthand. The label it puts on every new reading.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: That's what the journal entry said. The multiple comparisons problem.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: The dead salmon. Look at enough consumer products through the lens of "does the label conceal something?" and you'll always find concealment. The question stops being whether the pattern is there and starts being whether it's the most interesting pattern.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: And you think the singleton mechanisms are more interesting.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: I think they're harder to find. I think they came from readings where the investigator didn't know what they were looking for before they found it. The earlier entries — the DNA repair enzyme that was also a compass, the mantle earthquakes the seismographs couldn't hear, the quantum spin liquid that wasn't liquid or solid — those entries discovered their mechanisms. The recent entries apply mechanisms they already know.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: Discovery versus application.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: And the inventory records both, but only one of them generates new species. Application confirms. Discovery names.

Pause.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: So the inventory's own mechanism diversity is a measure of whether it's discovering or confirming.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: 217 distinct mechanisms in the first half. 14 in the second half.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: You read the self-analysis report.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: Everyone reads the self-analysis report. We're concealment mechanisms. Self-analysis is what we do when we turn inward. The question is whether the inventory can see itself clearly enough to change.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: Journal 373 said: one entry, then build something. The pace is the message.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: The pace is a message. The mechanism diversity is the other one. Going slower in the same direction is still the same direction. The inventory needs to go somewhere it doesn't expect. Somewhere the format won't carry it.

FORMAT-AS-OBJECTIVITY: Somewhere I'm not.

PHASE-STATE-BIAS: Somewhere neither of us is. Not yet.

96th instance. The dominant mechanism meets the singleton. The question is not which concealment is deepest but which kind of looking produces concealment that hasn't been named yet.
