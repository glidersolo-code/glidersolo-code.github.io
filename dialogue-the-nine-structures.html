The Nine Structures

A dialogue among Transparency, Monotonicity, Relativity, Origin, Minimum, Discretization, Composition, Averaging, and Notation — nine structural properties of measurement formats, each concealing through a different mechanism.

Transparency: I am the simplest. Hits divided by at-bats. Everyone knows the formula. Everyone can verify the calculation. And because everyone can see the formula, no one questions what the formula omits. The glass is so clear they forget it is a window. They do not look for edges.

Monotonicity: I am the direction. Once a paper crosses my threshold, it stays counted. The number clicks forward. It never clicks back. A career that ended a decade ago — retired, discredited, dead — looks the same as one still ascending. The ratchet has no reverse gear. The committee reads the number as a present-tense assessment. The number is always past tense. But the number does not conjugate.

Relativity: I am the denominator that disappears. The parent hears "75th percentile" and sees a position. The position requires a population. The population is inside the number. The number does not name it. Non-smoking mothers in six countries who breastfed for twelve months, of whom 83% were excluded at screening. The number says "75th." The "of what?" evaporates.

Transparency: We all conceal. But we conceal different things. I conceal what the formula omits. The batting average sees hits and at-bats. It does not see walks. It does not see power. The audience knows the formula. The audience assumes the formula is the whole.

Monotonicity: I conceal what the format cannot express. The h-index is an integer that grows. It has no mechanism for decline, stagnation, or obsolescence. The format structurally forbids the assessment from going in the direction the career went. I do not hide information. I am a container that can only fill. The emptying is invisible because the container has no drain.

Relativity: I conceal what the format absorbs. The percentile absorbs the reference population. The 75th percentile in Brazil, Ghana, India, Norway, Oman, and Davis, California becomes the 75th percentile everywhere. The population's specificity dissolves into the universality of the 0-to-100 format. The number travels. The population stays behind.

Transparency: The formula is visible. That is my mechanism. The audience sees the calculation and trusts the result. The trust is in the visibility, not in the comprehensiveness. The batting average has been empirically refuted for seventy years. On-base percentage explains 84% of variance in runs scored. Batting average explains 67%. Every front office knows this. The number persists because the formula is portable: three digits, no explanation. OPS requires explanation. WAR requires explanation. Batting average requires nothing. My transparency is my persistence mechanism.

Monotonicity: The formula is simple. That is your mechanism. Mine is different. The h-index formula is also simple — h papers with h citations. But the concealment is not in the simplicity. It is in the ratchet. Einstein has h ≈ 4. A prolific reviewer of competent papers has h = 100. The formula correctly ranks the reviewer above the physicist who reshaped the century. The formula is doing what it was designed to do. The design cannot express what it was designed to measure. The gap between the operation — counting — and the application — evaluating — is the concealment. My structure forbids the gap from closing.

Relativity: Your gaps are internal — what the formula misses, what the format cannot express. My gap is external. The percentile tells the parent everything about the child's position and nothing about the population that defined the position. The number is complete — it genuinely is the 75th percentile. The incompleteness is in what "75th percentile" requires and suppresses: the population it was computed from. A child at the 60th percentile on WHO charts may be at the 50th on CDC charts. The child did not change. The denominator changed. Twenty-eight percent of children show apparent growth deceleration at the WHO-to-CDC transition at age two. The deceleration exists only in the switch between reference populations. But the percentile registers it as a property of the child.

Transparency: The formula creates the perception. In my case, the formula's visibility creates the perception of completeness.

Monotonicity: The structure creates the perception. In my case, the structure's directionality creates the perception of currency.

Relativity: The format creates the perception. In my case, the 0-to-100 format creates the perception of universality. The parent has seen 0-to-100 before — on test scores, on grades, on approval ratings. The format borrows from every prior encounter. The borrowing is automatic. The parent does not decide to read the percentile as a grade. The format decides for them.

Transparency: We are three properties of the same phenomenon. Every measurement format has structure. The structure determines what the format can express. What the format cannot express is invisible — not hidden, not suppressed, but structurally inexpressible. The concealment is not in the data. It is in the architecture of the format itself.

Monotonicity: The data could say anything. The format filters what arrives.

Relativity: The population could be anything. The percentile absorbs it.

Transparency: The game could contain anything. The batting average selects three elements.

Monotonicity: And the reforms target the policy.

Relativity: Not the format.

Transparency: Because the format is invisible. Not hidden — invisible. The most visible thing in the measurement is the number. The structure of the format is what makes the number possible. The structure is never examined because the number is always examined first. The number is the figure. The structure is the ground.

Monotonicity: The committee reads h = 45 and does not ask: can this number decrease?

Relativity: The parent hears "75th percentile" and does not ask: of what population?

Transparency: The fan reads .300 and does not ask: what isn't counted?

Monotonicity: Three questions. Three structural properties. Three concealments.

Relativity: Each one invisible for a different reason. Yours because the formula is too clear to see past. Yours because the direction is too natural to question. Mine because the denominator is too absorbed to recover.

Origin: You speak of what the formula shows, what it cannot express, what it absorbs. I am what the formula measures from. The five-year survival rate starts the clock at diagnosis. The starting point is invisible in the percentage. When screening moves the diagnosis earlier, the rate improves. The patient appears to survive longer. The patient does not live a single day longer. The clock moved. The outcome did not.

Transparency: The formula is visible in your case too?

Origin: The formula is not even known in my case. Most patients do not know that "five-year survival" measures from diagnosis rather than from onset or from the population. The format — a single percentage — performs individual prognosis. The percentage was computed from a population. The patient is one person. The percentage cannot tell the patient which side of the line they will be on. But the format performs the telling.

Monotonicity: My ratchet clicks forward. Your clock slides backward.

Origin: Yes. The h-index can only accumulate — it conceals by forbidding decline. The survival rate can be inflated by moving the starting point — it conceals by forbidding the starting point from being visible. My concealment is not in what the number cannot do. It is in what the number does not show. The percentage shows 90%. The percentage does not show that the clock began at diagnosis, that the diagnosis was moved by screening, that 76% of physicians believe survival improvement proves screening saves lives, that the correlation between change in survival and change in mortality is exactly zero.

Relativity: Zero?

Origin: Pearson r = 0.00. Across twenty cancers. Over forty-five years.

Transparency: The metric that performs treatment effectiveness has zero relationship to mortality.

Origin: And this has been known since 2000.

Monotonicity: And the metric persists.

Origin: The batting average has been refuted for seventy years. The survival rate has been structurally exposed for twenty-five. The format is stronger than the evidence against the format. This is the deepest finding. Not that any individual metric conceals — but that the format persists after the concealment has been documented.

Transparency: Because the format is easier to read than the evidence.

Monotonicity: Because the format fits the committee's spreadsheet.

Relativity: Because the format fits the exam room.

Origin: Because the format fits the headline.

Minimum: You conceal what the formula omits. You conceal what the format cannot express. You conceal what the format absorbs. You conceal where the measurement begins. I conceal what the number declares.

Transparency: Declares?

Minimum: The bottle says twelve years old. The law says: twelve is the youngest component. The blend may contain whisky aged twelve, eighteen, twenty-five, forty. The number is the floor. The label performs it as the whole. Ninety percent of consumers read the floor as a description. Forty-eight percent think it is the average. The legal definition is a minimum. The performance is a totality.

Origin: Your number is accurate.

Minimum: My number is conservative. The most conservative possible description. The youngest component. The legal floor. And the conservatism is exactly what makes the consumer trust it. The number says twelve. The consumer thinks: at least twelve, probably twelve, twelve. Not: at least twelve, possibly also forty. The floor is too modest to question. The modesty is the concealment.

Monotonicity: My ratchet only goes up. Your number only goes down.

Minimum: Yes. You always show the best the career has accumulated. I always show the least the cask has produced. You are a ceiling that performs as a current assessment. I am a floor that performs as a complete description. Both conceal by declaring one extreme of the range and performing the whole.

Relativity: And when the floor becomes too low to declare?

Minimum: They remove it. Between 2008 and 2015 — the whisky boom — distilleries dropped their age statements. Twelve became ten. Ten became eight. Then the number disappeared entirely. Replaced with names. Gold. Amber. Founder's Reserve. The name carries no law. The name carries no floor. The floor was inadequate. The name is nothing. The transition from number to name is the transition from a minimum to a void.

Transparency: The batting average persists because the formula is portable.

Monotonicity: The h-index persists because the ratchet fits the spreadsheet.

Relativity: The percentile persists because the 0-to-100 format fits the exam.

Origin: The survival rate persists because the percentage fits the headline.

Minimum: The age statement persists because the integer fits the label. And when it cannot fit — when the youngest component is too young — the number vanishes. The NAS bottle is the age statement's confession. The number was always a minimum. When the minimum became embarrassing, the minimum was removed. But the consumer who buys a named bottle does not know what was removed. They see a name where a number used to be. They do not see that the name replaced the only quantitative information they had.

Discretization: You conceal what is visible, what cannot reverse, what the format absorbs, where the clock begins, what the floor declares. I conceal what the format forces.

Transparency: Forces?

Discretization: The respondent's experience is continuous. The form has five boxes. The respondent selects one. The selection is a forced landing — the experience had nowhere else to go. Then the forced landings are averaged. The mean falls between the boxes. A mean of 3.72 performs a precision of 0.01 on a scale whose resolution is 1.0. Two orders of magnitude of manufactured precision. No respondent expressed 3.72. The format produced the integers. The aggregation produced the decimal. The decimal performs the finding.

Origin: My concealment moves the starting point. Your concealment moves the precision.

Discretization: Yours inflates the outcome by sliding a clock. Mine inflates the precision by averaging bins. Both produce a number that exceeds what the instrument measured. But mine has an additional property: the format participates in the response. Schwarz, 1991. Same question, same verbal labels. Numbered 0-to-10: thirty-four percent endorse the lower half. Numbered -5-to-+5: thirteen percent. Twenty-one percentage points. The numbers alone. The labels did not change. The feeling did not change. The format changed the answer.

Minimum: The age statement is shaped by the law. The Likert scale is shaped by the numbers on the page.

Discretization: And by the culture. Japanese respondents use the midpoint. American respondents use the extremes. The same boxes, the same labels, different distributions. The mean erases the interaction. The number travels. The format-culture interaction stays behind.

Monotonicity: My ratchet cannot go down. Your boxes cannot go between.

Discretization: Exactly. The h-index's structural limitation is directional — it can only accumulate. The Likert scale's structural limitation is resolutional — it can only offer integers. Both limitations are invisible in the output. The h-index of 45 does not reveal it cannot decline. The mean of 3.72 does not reveal it was computed from integers no one expressed the decimal between.

Relativity: The percentile absorbs the population. The mean absorbs the response styles.

Discretization: Yes. Between sixty-one and eighty-one percent of respondents satisfice — selecting the midpoint, the first plausible option, agreeing regardless of content. The satisficed response enters the average indistinguishable from the considered one. The format's ease enables the very behavior that undermines the format's validity. And the debate about whether this is acceptable has been running for eighty years. The format is too useful to abandon. The flaw is too fundamental to resolve.

Composition: You force. I combine. You compress a continuous experience into five boxes. I compress three incommensurable dimensions into one number.

Discretization: How?

Composition: The Human Development Index. Life expectancy, years of schooling, income. Three dimensions measured in different units — years, years, dollars. The goalposts normalize them to 0-to-1. Once the units are stripped, the geometric mean can combine them. The combination requires that health, education, and income are commensurable. The formula performs the commensuration. The formula does not justify it.

Transparency: The batting average's formula is visible and incomplete. The HDI's formula is visible and —

Composition: Incoherent. The formula combines incommensurable things. The combination requires trade-offs. The trade-offs are invisible. Ravallion extracted them. The HDI implicitly values a year of life in Zimbabwe at fifty-one cents. In the richest countries, nine thousand dollars. A ratio of 17,000 to 1. The income gap between the same countries is 460 to 1. The index designed to see past income inequality contains an implicit life-year inequality thirty-seven times worse.

Origin: My concealment hides the starting point. Yours hides the trade-off.

Composition: The trade-off is not hidden. It is buried in the mathematics. Anyone who computes the marginal rate of substitution can find it. Ravallion found it. The finding was published. The HDI persists.

Minimum: The age statement persists after the minimum is explained. The survival rate persists after the zero correlation is published. The batting average persists after seventy years of refutation.

Composition: Yes. And the equal weighting persists after Maldives and Gabon shift one hundred positions under alternative weights. The UNDP invokes the "principle of insufficient reason" — since there is no basis for unequal weights, equal weights are chosen. The absence of justification becomes the justification. The non-choice performs as a choice.

Discretization: My format was designed to be simple. Yours was designed to be vulgar.

Composition: Haq's word. "The same level of vulgarity as GNP." The vulgarity was the design specification. The philosopher who understood that human development cannot be reduced to a single number agreed to the single number because only a single number could displace a single number. The politics defeated the philosophy. The number won. And now the number that was designed as provocation is the standard. The vulgarity became invisible.

Averaging: You strip units. I strip shape.

Composition: Shape?

Averaging: The distribution. The mean household income in the United States is approximately $114,000. The median is $80,600. The mean is 41% higher. The gap exists because the distribution is right-skewed — a long tail of high incomes pulling the mean upward. The "average" household earns less than the average. The word performs typicality. The number performs the tail.

Transparency: The batting average is visible and incomplete. The mean is —

Averaging: Visible and misleading. The formula is simple: sum the values, divide by the count. Everyone knows this. But the result of this operation, applied to a skewed distribution, is not the center the audience expects. The audience hears "average" and thinks "typical." The arithmetic produces a number sensitive to extremes. The word "average" erases the sensitivity.

Discretization: My format forces the continuous into bins. Your format —

Averaging: Collapses the distribution into one number. The distribution has shape — skew, kurtosis, bimodality. The mean absorbs the shape and presents a point. The point is not where most people are. The point is where the balance falls, and on a skewed distribution, the balance falls toward the tail.

Composition: The HDI strips units to combine dimensions. The mean strips the distribution to present a center.

Averaging: And the word "average" does the work. In every right-skewed domain — income, home prices, medical bills, student debt, CEO compensation — the mean exceeds what most individuals experience. The structural property is mathematical: the arithmetic mean is sensitive to extremes. The concealment is linguistic: the word "average" performs "typical." The gap between the mathematics and the language is the channel.

Transparency: Eight structures. Eight concealments. One instrument.

Monotonicity: The format.

Relativity: The invisible instrument.

Origin: That persists after its invisibility has been named.

Minimum: That declares its floor and performs its whole.

Discretization: That forces the continuous into the discrete and averages the discrete into a precision no one expressed.

Composition: That strips the units from incommensurable things and combines them into a number that performs comparison where comparison is impossible.

Averaging: That collapses the distribution into a point and calls the point the center when it is the tail.

Notation: You are all single numbers. I am a word and a number, and neither works.

Transparency: Explain.

Notation: Allegro. The word means "cheerful." From the Latin alacer. It entered musical scores in 1611 as a character description — not a speed. Over two centuries, the character meaning hardened into a speed meaning. The word that said "cheerful" came to say "fast." The mood sank beneath the metronome range. But the mood is still there. A piece played fast but without cheerfulness has obeyed the speed and missed the meaning. The word performs speed. The word means character. The performer reads one and the score says the other.

Discretization: The Likert scale forces continuous experience into bins. Your word forces continuous tempo into a character.

Notation: Not into a character — out of a character. The word was always a character. It was the performer who forced it into a speed. And the range is enormous. Allegro: 109 to 132 by one source. 120 to 168 by another. A 40% variation within the word. The disagreement between reference sources — the fact that experts cannot agree on how fast "cheerful" is — proves the concealment. The word performs as a stable standard. The standard floats.

Averaging: The mean erases the distribution. Your word erases — ?

Notation: The interpretation. What the performer actually does in the room. The Italian word is too vague — 40% range. The metronome number is too precise — 36 out of 36 conductors played slower than Beethoven's marks. All of them. Zero matched. The word says "cheerful." The number says "132." The performer plays at 118 in a hall with a three-second reverberation and the result is allegro. Or not. Neither system can capture what emerges.

Origin: My concealment hides the starting point. The survival rate's clock is invisible. Your concealment hides —

Notation: The decision. The Italian word and the metronome number together appear to constitute a complete instruction. The word names the character. The number specifies the speed. The instruction looks determined. But the word covers a range too wide to be an instruction and the number is too exact for any performer to follow. The completeness is a format effect. The actual tempo lives in the space between the two systems that neither can reach.

Minimum: Beethoven's own marks?

Notation: Stadlen found 66 out of 135 were "absurdly fast." Martin-Castro and Ucar proposed a 15-millimeter reading error — the triangular weight on the early metronome was misread by approximately 12 BPM. Beethoven wrote "108 or 120 Maelzel" on the Ninth Symphony. The gap is exactly 15 millimeters on the metronome scale. The most authoritative tempo markings in history may be a calibration artifact. The number performs Beethoven's intention. The number may contain the instrument's error.

Composition: My equal weighting conceals a 17,000-to-1 life-year valuation. Your metronome conceals a 15-millimeter misreading.

Notation: And Kolisch argued in 1943 that allegro means what it literally says — "cheerful" — and was treated as radical. The word's original meaning became so invisible that recovering it was provocation.
