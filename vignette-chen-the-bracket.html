The Bracket

Maren's coworker turned down a promotion.

"She said the raise would put her in a higher bracket," Maren says, scrolling her phone at the kitchen counter. "She said she'd actually take home less."

Chen is drying a mug. "How much was the raise?"

"Three thousand. She's at forty-six now, would have gone to forty-nine."

Chen sets the mug down. "She'd pay 22% on the three thousand above the threshold. Not on everything. The first eleven thousand is still at ten. The next thirty-five at twelve. Only the last eighteen hundred enters the new bracket."

"I know that."

"She doesn't."

"One in three Americans doesn't. I saw a survey." Maren puts the phone down. "But here's what I keep thinking — why doesn't the form just show it? The effective rate. The actual percentage. Total tax divided by total income. One number that answers the question everyone is asking."

Chen picks up another mug. "The table shows ranges and rates. Like a classification system. Like blood types. You're in one group."

"But you're not in one group."

"You're in every group below. The first eleven thousand is always in the ten-percent layer, whether you earn twenty thousand or two hundred thousand. The layers accumulate. The table doesn't show accumulation. The table shows assignment."

Maren is quiet. Then: "Her supervisor asked her to reconsider. She said no. She was certain. The table told her she'd lose money. The table was right about the rate and wrong about how the rate works."

"The visible number is the wrong answer to the right question," Chen says. "The invisible number is the right answer. The right answer has no line on any form."

"She stayed at forty-six."

"The format kept her there."

Maren picks up her phone again. Puts it down. "The word 'bracket' — it's doing something. A bracket encloses. What's inside is treated the same."

"In every other context. Typography, tournaments, shelving."

"The tax bracket borrows the word and borrows the enclosure. But it doesn't enclose. It layers."

Chen folds the towel. "The word performs the containment. The system doesn't contain. The word wins."

"She's good at her job."

"The format doesn't care."
