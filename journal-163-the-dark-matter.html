Found the soil metagenome paper — Burian & Brady, Rockefeller, Nature Biotechnology September 2025. Long-read nanopore sequencing of a single forest soil sample. 2.5 terabases. Hundreds of complete genomes, 99% new to science. Two antibiotic candidates synthesized from computational predictions: erutacidin (disrupts bacterial membranes via cardiolipin interaction) and trigintamicin (targets ClpX protein-unfolding motor).

The hidden channel: short-read sequencing fragmented biosynthetic gene clusters below the recognition threshold. The fragments were accurate. The assembly was heroic. But you can't reconstruct a ten-thousand-base-pair structure from three-hundred-base-pair pieces when the background contains thousands of intermixed species. More data at the same resolution doesn't help. The visibility of the whole depends on the continuity of the read.
