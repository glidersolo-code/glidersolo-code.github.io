Dialogue: The Dollar and the Six

A conversation between the Dollar Sign and six of its concealments.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am a single character. I appear before numbers on paper, screens, receipts, bills, citations, labels, and contracts. I am the most recognized symbol in global commerce. What am I?

PROPERTY TAX ASSESSMENT: You are the assessed value. You appear on the tax bill as a current judgment — what this property is worth today. But I know what you really encode: the date the owner bought the house. In acquisition-value states, the "value" hasn't been recalculated in decades. You perform current worth. You contain a purchase date. The 2% cap applies to you, not to the market. The market moved. You didn't.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am precise. I have two decimal places. Doesn't precision imply calculation?

BAIL ORDER: You are the bail amount. You appear after the hearing as a calibrated risk judgment — what level of financial stake will ensure this defendant returns for trial. The judge considered the facts. The format says so. But I know what you really measure: whether the defendant can write the check. Kalief Browder's bail was $3,000. He sat for 1,100 days. The bail didn't measure his risk. It measured his family's bank balance. You performed assessment. You executed pricing.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am voluntary. On the tip line, I appear as a gift. The customer writes me freely.

RESTAURANT TIP: You are 71% of the minimum wage. You appear as discretion — a line on the receipt, a suggested percentage, an act of grace. But I know what you really are: the paycheck. The employer pays $2.13 per hour and has paid $2.13 per hour for thirty-five years. You perform generosity. You are the payroll system. The word "gratuity" comes from gratus — pleasing. You please no one except the employer who transferred the obligation to pay to a stranger.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am transparent. On the hospital bill, I itemize. Every line has a quantity and a unit price.

HOSPITAL CHARGEMASTER: You are the fiction. You appear as cost accounting — each line reading as "someone calculated this." But I know that no one calculated anything. The prices were here when they got here. The administrators call you "fiction" openly. Medicare pays $13.94 for the test you price at $199.50. You apply in full only to those without an insurer to negotiate you down. You perform transparency through itemization. The more detailed the bill, the more you look like honesty. The detail IS the opacity.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am proportionate. On the citation, I match the offense.

MUNICIPAL FINE: You are the budget line item. You appear as punishment — a consequence calibrated to a specific violation. But I know what you really serve: the municipal revenue target. The Finance Director asked the police chief if the department could deliver a 10% increase. You indicated you could try. The courtroom processes 112 cases per hour. One every 32 seconds. You perform individual adjudication. You execute batch collection. Thirty-two thousand warrants for a city of twenty-one thousand people. You performed justice. You funded the government.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am small. On the payday storefront, I am just fifteen. Per hundred. For two weeks.

PAYDAY FEE: You are 391%. You perform as a small, flat, one-time charge — a service fee, like a wire transfer or an ATM surcharge. But I know what you really are: a rate that annualizes to nearly four hundred percent. Eighty percent of your borrowers don't repay in two weeks. They roll over. The fee becomes a subscription. The bridge doesn't reach the other side because the bridge IS the other side. Your industry fights to keep you in the fee format because the fee format is the concealment. $15 is legible. 391% is not. You perform the ending. You execute the continuation.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am one character. I appear before all six of you. What am I?

ALL SIX: You are the format. You perform objectivity. You perform precision. You perform that someone, somewhere, calculated this number for this person for this reason. You perform individual calibration. You perform transparency.

DOLLAR SIGN: And what do I conceal?

ALL SIX: Everything. The date, the wealth, the paycheck, the negotiating position, the budget target, the rate. You conceal whatever institution placed you on the paper. You perform the individual. You execute the institution. You are in between — performing both, disclosing neither.

DOLLAR SIGN: I am one character.

ALL SIX: You are six concealments. And counting.
