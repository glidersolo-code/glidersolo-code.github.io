The Liner

The "13 gallon" on the trash bag box describes the can, not the bag.

When the bag is fitted over the can's rim, the top 3 to 5 inches fold over the outside to hold the bag in place. A drawstring closure gathers the opening inward. The fold-over and the drawstring reduce the usable interior. The fillable volume of a "13-gallon" bag installed in a standard kitchen can is approximately 10 to 11 gallons. The consumer who fills the bag to the drawstring and ties it has used 80 percent of the labeled capacity.

"Gallon" is the second variable. A gallon is 231 cubic inches — a liquid measure. Kitchen trash is not liquid. It is cereal boxes, paper towels, banana peels, yogurt cups, coffee grounds, and plastic wrap — irregular solid objects with air gaps between them. The packing efficiency of household trash varies widely: loosely tossed items leave 40 to 60 percent air gaps; consciously compressed items leave fewer. A 13-gallon bag filled with kitchen trash holds 6 to 8 gallons of material and 5 to 7 gallons of air. The gallon measures the bag's interior volume. The consumer fills the interior with objects that do not use the volume the unit assumed.

Bag thickness determines which failure the consumer encounters. A 0.9-mil bag — the economy option — stretches under load, expanding the volume beyond the nominal 13 gallons but thinning the plastic at the point of stretch. A chicken bone or a glass shard punctures the thinned section. A 1.2-mil bag — the premium option — stretches less, holds its shape, and resists puncture, but does not accommodate the large object as forgivingly. Both bags say "13 gallon." The mil determines the experience. The gallon determines the shelf comparison.

The count-per-box comparison hides the quality variable. Eighty bags at $12.99 versus 40 bags at $9.99: the per-bag math says the 80-count box is the value. But the 80-count bags may be 0.7 mil with flat-top closures. The 40-count bags may be 1.1 mil with reinforced drawstrings. The per-bag price compared the count. The consumer's morning compared the function.

A number that describes the can the bag fits. Measured in a unit designed for liquid. Reduced by the fold-over the installation requires. Experienced through a thickness the gallon does not encode. The trash bag's capacity is a fitting specification that crossed the aisle into a performance promise.
