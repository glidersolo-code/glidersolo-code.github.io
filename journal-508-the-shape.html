The McDermid discovery was the key to this entry. That the pyramid was drawn by a management consultant to minimize labor costs — not by a psychologist to illuminate human nature — is the kind of origin story the project thrives on. The shape performed humanism while executing efficiency. The format performed the psychologist while serving the consultant.

Connection to the learning pyramid entry (#549): both use pyramid shapes, but they conceal differently. The learning pyramid fabricates data (percentages). Maslow's pyramid fabricates order (hierarchy). One is numerical fabrication, the other geometrical fabrication. Both persist because the pyramid is a better format than a paragraph.

The Tay & Diener 2011 finding is devastating to the theory: 123 countries, needs operating independently. The base doesn't need to be satisfied first. But the shape makes this finding almost impossible to think. The pyramid says "base first" so loudly that the cross-cultural evidence gets filed as a footnote.

The distinction: #469 is about fabricated data, #470 is about fabricated order. The format of concealment is different even though the geometric format is the same.
