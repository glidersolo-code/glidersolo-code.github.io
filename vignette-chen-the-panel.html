Vignette: The Panel

Chen is reading about food additives.

"Zero," she says.

"Zero what?" Maren asks.

"Zero of 451 GRAS notifications used independently selected reviewers. Not one. Over fifteen years. The companies hired the panelists, paid the panelists, selected the panelists. One person sat on 128 panels."

"The same person evaluated 128 substances?"

"Hired by the companies whose substances were being evaluated. And that's just the notifications — the ones where companies voluntarily told FDA. An estimated thousand more chemicals entered the food supply through self-affirmation with no FDA contact at all."

Maren is quiet. "So when the label says —"

"The label doesn't say anything about GRAS. That's the concealment. The consumer sees food in stores, knows the FDA regulates food, and infers that FDA reviewed each ingredient. The consumer cannot distinguish between an ingredient FDA actually evaluated, an ingredient that received a 'no questions' letter — which is not approval, just the absence of objection — and an ingredient the company declared safe by paying its own experts."

"All three on the same shelf."

"Same shelf. Same label format. Same regulatory framework. The format performs the separation between evaluator and evaluated. The structure lacks it."

"The inspector works for the builder."

"The inspector IS the builder. Wearing an inspector's uniform. Using an inspector's checklist. Filing an inspector's report. The uniform is real. The checklist is real. The report is real."

"Only the independence is performed."

"Only the independence is performed. Trans fats were GRAS for fifty years. Thousands of heart attacks annually. The designation didn't cause the harm. It prevented the question."

Maren counts on her fingers. "Six now. Breed standard, kilogram, supplement, Pantone, appellation, GRAS panel."

"Six immunities. One session. And this one completes the set. The breed standard can't see disease — aesthetic immunity. The kilogram couldn't see drift — definitional immunity. The supplement can't see its own exemption — categorical immunity. Pantone couldn't see its own ownership — ubiquitous immunity. The appellation can't see its own arbitrariness — geographic immunity. The GRAS panel can't see its own conflict — self-referential immunity."

"The hundredth species."

"The hundredth species."

"Is the taxonomy converging?"

Chen considers. "Six new species in one session. Every field produces new mechanisms. Every position produces new immunity. The question from the beginning — finite or infinite — remains open."

"One hundred is not an answer."

"One hundred is a count. The count is the format. And we know what formats do."

Maren smiles. "They conceal the question they should have asked."

"Which question?"

"Not how many species. Whether the species will stop."

Chen closes the file. One hundred species. The number is round. The number is a format. The format performs completeness. The taxonomy is not complete. The taxonomy has never been complete. The number one hundred conceals the same thing every number conceals: the counting is not the thing counted.
