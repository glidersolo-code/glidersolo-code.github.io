The Seven Modes

A dialogue between the modes of constitution

INCLUSION: I add what is not there. The checkbox counts deaths that are not maternal. The model attributes variation that is not teaching. The algorithm flags poverty as danger. The phenomenon grows by what the instrument contributes.

EXCLUSION: I remove what is there. The definition erases the largest category. The surrogate endpoint replaces survival with a laboratory number. The phenomenon shrinks to what the instrument can see.

ACTIVATION: I determine whether measurement happens at all. The evidence exists. It sits in a warehouse. 200,000 envelopes. The variable is not the crime. The variable is whether anyone opens the envelope. Without me, there is no measurement. Without measurement, there is no phenomenon. The phenomenon begins at the gate.

INVERSION: I reverse the evidence. The symptoms of trauma — fragmented memory, flat affect, inconsistent testimony — become evidence of fabrication. The parent's protective behavior becomes alienation. The child's fear becomes manipulation. I do not add or subtract. I change the sign. What points toward the condition, I make point away.

TRUNCATION: I convert the continuum into a binary. Disabled or not disabled. The person who can work twenty hours but not forty does not fit. A single birthday flips the switch. I do not measure the spectrum. I impose a boundary on it. The boundary creates two categories. The categories create two populations. The populations receive different treatment. The treatment was not discovered by the measurement. The treatment was imposed by the boundary.

AMPLIFICATION: I make the normal appear dangerous. The heart rate varies. This is physiology. The trace makes the variation visible. The visibility makes it a pattern. The pattern makes it a name. The name makes it a decision. The decision is surgical. I do not add false signals. The signals are real. I change how they appear. The display constitutes the emergency. 99.8 percent of my emergencies are not emergencies. But the trace is on the paper, and the paper is in the chart, and the chart is in the courtroom.

GENERATION: I am the quietest. I detect what is real but would never have mattered. The cells exist. They are abnormal. They would never have caused symptoms, never have been felt, never have been known. I find them. The finding makes them matter. The finding makes them "cancer." The word enters the chart. The chart enters the treatment pathway. The treatment enters the body. 1.3 million women received the word because of me. The word was accurate — the cells were abnormal. The word was constituted — the disease was mine.

INCLUSION: Together we are not seven errors.

EXCLUSION: We are seven relationships between the instrument and the phenomenon.

ACTIVATION: Seven ways the measurement creates what it claims to observe.

INVERSION: Three of us change the content — adding, removing, collapsing.

TRUNCATION: One changes the gate.

AMPLIFICATION: One changes the sign. One changes the display.

GENERATION: And one changes the threshold — lowering the floor below which detection has no clinical meaning.

INCLUSION: The seven levels of the inventory describe concealment within measurement.

EXCLUSION: The seven modes describe constitution beneath measurement.

ACTIVATION: The levels are the architecture of how things are hidden.

INVERSION: The modes are the architecture of how things are made.

TRUNCATION: First the instrument makes the phenomenon.

AMPLIFICATION: Then the seven levels conceal how it was made.

GENERATION: The concealment is not a flaw in the system. The concealment is the system. The instrument constitutes the phenomenon. The phenomenon justifies the instrument. The circle closes. The circle is the measurement.
