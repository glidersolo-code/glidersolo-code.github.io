The Linearity

Chen drew a line on the napkin. Straight. Through the origin.

"Linear no-threshold. Risk is proportional to dose. No safe level. The line that governs radiation protection worldwide."

"Where does the data support it?"

"Above 100 millisieverts. Hiroshima survivors. Medical exposures. The dose-response is clear up there. Below 100 mSv—" Chen pointed to the lower-left corner of the line. "The signal disappears. The epidemiological data cannot be distinguished from background cancer rates."

"So the line below 100 mSv is—"

"Geometry. Not biology. An extrapolation from a data-rich region into a data-absent region. The line is continuous, so the eye does not see where measurement ends and assumption begins."

"And Muller?"

"Nobel Prize lecture, December 1946. Called the relationship 'undoubtedly linear with no threshold.' One month earlier, Caspari had completed a study showing a threshold in chronically irradiated Drosophila. Muller had seen the data. He did not mention it."

Maren set down her coffee. "He suppressed a threshold finding."

"He gave a Nobel lecture without disclosing a relevant contradictory result he had reviewed weeks earlier. The NAS BEAR I Panel adopted LNT in 1956 with Muller as a key member. From there: ICRP, ALARA, NRC, EPA, WHO, IAEA. The line entered every regulatory framework on the planet."

"Fukushima."

"Zero confirmed radiation fatalities among the general population. UNSCEAR's conclusion. 164,000 evacuated. 1,600 evacuation-related deaths. The model predicted deaths at doses where no deaths were demonstrated. The evacuation — driven by the model — killed 1,600 people fleeing a dose that harmed no one."

"So the line is wrong?"

"The line may be wrong below 100 mSv. The French Academy said so in 2005 — 'incompatible with the postulate.' The American BEIR VII said it was right, the same year. The question is not whether the line is wrong. The question is why it persists."

"Because it's load-bearing."

"ALARA depends on LNT. If there is a safe threshold, you minimize to the threshold and stop. ALARA says minimize always. Remove linearity, remove ALARA. Remove ALARA, restructure every radiation regulation in the world. Evacuation zones change. Decommissioning standards change. Waste classification changes. Nuclear energy policy changes."

Maren looked at the napkin. The parabola from MSY. The multiplication sign from QALY. The exponent from the discount rate. Now the straight line from LNT.

"Four models," she said. "Four mathematical forms. Four policies."

"The parabola generates the fisheries quota. The multiplication generates the healthcare allocation. The exponent generates the intergenerational valuation. The straight line generates the evacuation order. Each model performs as a measurement. Each is a decision. Each outlived its own critique."

"Larkin's epitaph. The ADA rejection. Ramsey's condemnation. The French Academy report."

"1977. 1992. 1928. 2005. The quota is set. The multiplication runs. The exponent compounds. The line extends. The models are the policies. The critiques are the footnotes."

Maren traced the line on the napkin. It passed through the origin with the confidence of a function that has never paused to wonder whether it was biology or geometry.

"The data stops at 100 millisieverts," she said. "The line doesn't."

"Nothing about the line knows where the data ends. That's the fourth model. The form of the extrapolation is the regulation."
