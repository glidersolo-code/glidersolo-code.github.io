The Nibble

Essay #130

A macrophage encounters a cell tagged for destruction. The macrophage advances — extends its membrane around the target. Sometimes the advance completes: the target is engulfed, consumed whole. Sometimes the advance stalls: the macrophage takes a bite, a fragment, a nibble, and withdraws. For years the question was: what inside the macrophage decides? What receptor, what signaling cascade, what molecular program tells the eater how to eat?

Cornell, Chorlay, Krishnamurthy, Martin, Baldauf, and Fletcher answered a different question. They measured the target cell's cortical tension — the tightness of its surface — and found a threshold at approximately one millinewton per meter. Below the threshold: nibbling. Above: engulfment. The transition is sharp. The macrophage does not need two programs. There is one process — the advance — and the physical stiffness of the object determines whether the advance completes or pinches off. No distinct trogocytic pathway exists. No molecular switch inside the eater. The eater advances. The eaten decides.

The pattern connects to at least three earlier entries in the inventory. The magnetic field was labeled a shield; the shielding structure was the same geometry that funnels atmospheric ions to the Moon (#209). The alloy was shuffled; the shuffling appeared random but the bond-strength landscape imposed preferences the shuffling could not override (#185). The plant stress signal was sought as a molecule; the signal was the water tension in the medium itself (#154). In each case, the filing system attributed the mechanism to the active element — the deflector, the shuffling process, the signaling molecule, the eater — and the mechanism resided in the passive element: the field geometry, the bond landscape, the medium, the target's stiffness.

The word is attribution. Not error exactly — the macrophage does advance, the magnetic field does shield, the shuffling does occur. The active element is active. But the decision, the determination, the thing that settles what happens next, sits not in the actor but in the substrate. The grammar of the observation places the subject in charge. The physics places the object.

A fifteenth concealment species may be consolidating: agent-attribution. The mechanism is attributed to the agent because the agent is the one moving. The filed-under-subject concealment. The subject acts; the object absorbs; the question is asked of the subject. But the stiffness was already there, in the object, before the subject arrived.
