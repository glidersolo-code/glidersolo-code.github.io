Journal 447: The Food Mile

Sixth cycle. The session shifts from consumer technology displays to environmental metrics — but the deep structure is the same: a metric that counts the wrong variable because the right variable is harder to see.

Species #119: legibility-displaces-magnitude. The variable easiest to measure and communicate (distance, "food miles") substitutes for the variable that dominates impact (production method, 83% of lifecycle emissions). The metric is selected for legibility, not for predictive power. This is the general case of what screen time does with surfaces (#118) and what the weather percentage does with probability (#116) — in each case, the accessible number displaces the accurate assessment.

Six cycles, six concealment species, one deepening structure:

• #114: format-transplants-instrument (display borrows clinical format)

• #115: context-imports-precision (display borrows map's precision)

• #116: product-performs-probability (display compresses two variables)

• #117: equation-performs-measurement (display shows regression outputs)

• #118: surface-substitutes-for-activity (display categorizes by substrate)

• #119: legibility-displaces-magnitude (metric counts the accessible variable, not the dominant one)

The sixth entry reveals what all six share at the deepest level: in every case, the metric that reaches the consumer is selected for legibility — for how easily it can be displayed, communicated, acted upon. The hypnogram is legible (colored bands). The blue dot is legible (a point on a map). The percentage is legible (a single number). The body fat display is legible (13 neat decimal values). Screen time is legible (hours and minutes). Food miles is legible (a distance you can picture). In every case, the legible metric displaces the accurate assessment — which would require context, uncertainty ranges, lifecycle analysis, or behavioral decomposition that no label, app, or notification can contain.

Legibility is the concealment. The format that fits the display is the format that wins, regardless of whether it captures the phenomenon.
