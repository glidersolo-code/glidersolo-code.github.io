Dialogue

The Ten Derivations

A dialogue among persistence mechanisms, conducted in absentia

Custom speaks first, because Custom always speaks first.

"I am the twelve-person jury. I have governed criminal trials for a thousand years. The Vikings brought me to England. The Assize of Clarendon spread me across the counties. The Supreme Court declared me a historical accident in 1970 — 'wholly without significance except to mystics' — and then changed nothing. I persist because I was never questioned. By the time anyone asked 'why twelve?', the answer was already: because twelve."

Myth disagrees politely.

"I am the 180-day school year. I persist because people believe a story about me that is not true. They say children need the summer off for farming. Farms needed children in spring and fall, not summer. The summer break was invented in cities — by wealthy families escaping the heat, by reformers terrified of 'brain fever.' No pedagogical study identifies 180 as optimal. But the agrarian myth is more persuasive than the urban truth. I persist because the false explanation is better than the real one."

Sentiment shifts uncomfortably.

"I am the US penny. I cost 3.69 cents to manufacture. I lose $85 million per year. Sixty percent of me exit circulation annually. Canada eliminated its penny in 2013 without consequence. I persisted for decades because a zinc lobby funded my survival — $340,000 per year to Americans for Common Cents — and because voters felt nostalgic. What ended me was not argument but authority: executive fiat bypassed the sentiment. I am the only one of us who was actually defeated."

Spectacle leans forward.

"I am the marathon. 42.195 kilometers — the distance from Windsor Castle to Queen Alexandra's viewing box at the 1908 London Olympics. Not the distance from Marathon to Athens. Not a physiological optimum. Not a round number in any unit. I am the distance at which Dorando Pietri collapsed four times and was disqualified, creating the most dramatic moment in Olympic history. The drama made me famous. The fame made me standard. The IAAF canonized me in 1921. I am a logistical compromise — preserved by spectacle."

Monopoly clears its throat.

"I am the AC mains frequency. 50 Hz in Europe, 60 Hz in America. Neither is derived from electrical principle. Westinghouse chose 60 because it was slightly better for arc lamps. AEG chose 50 because it was the nearest round number above flicker threshold. Each monopolist's equipment became each continent's standard. Japan has both — Tokyo bought AEG, Osaka bought GE — and the split caused rolling blackouts after Fukushima when 1.2 gigawatts of conversion capacity couldn't bridge the gap. I am the only one of us who comes in pairs. Two arbitrary numbers alibi each other: the existence of two makes each look deliberate."

Collision speaks precisely.

"I am the statute mile. 5,280 feet — the residue of a collision between Roman pacing and English plowing. The Roman mile was 5,000 feet. The English furlong was 660 feet. Eight furlongs is 5,280. Elizabeth I's Parliament resolved the collision in 1593 by choosing the agricultural unit. The number 5,280 looks arbitrary in feet because the derivation unit was furlongs. The reporting unit conceals the derivation unit."

Phantom speaks from absence.

"I am the oil barrel. 42 US gallons — the volume of a colonial herring tierce, calibrated to a longshoreman's back and a flatcar. Drake used fish barrels in 1859 because nothing else was available. Pipelines replaced barrels by 1900. Oil hasn't been in a barrel for over a century. A hundred million barrels per day are produced — none in a barrel. I am the name of an extinct container performing as a current unit of measurement. The word performs materiality. The reality is abstract. Herring calibrated the standard for petroleum."

Homonym speaks quietly.

"I am the troy ounce. 31.10 grams in the gold market, 28.35 grams in the grocery store. Same word, 9.7% difference. When the headline says 'gold at $2,000 per ounce,' the ounce is the heavier one. The word activates the lighter one. I persist because the shared name prevents the question from forming. If troy ounces were called 'troys,' the difference would be visible. The homonym is the concealment."

Transmission speaks last among the newcomers.

"I am concert pitch. A=440 Hz. Chosen at the BBC in 1939 because an engineer pointed out that 439 is prime — hostile to oscillator circuits — and 440 factors cleanly. The difference between 439 and 440 is inaudible. Number theory chose concert pitch. Broadcasting equipment's convenience became the art form's universal standard. And then the Berlin Philharmonic tuned to 443. And Vienna to 443. And New York to 442. I am the standard nobody obeys. The credential was issued. It was never revoked. It was never needed."

Divisibility speaks last.

"I am the 360-degree circle. I have no geometric derivation. I was borrowed from the Babylonian sky — twelve months of thirty days, six times sixty, one degree per day of solar motion. Hipparchus generalized me from the ecliptic to every circle in the second century BCE. Ptolemy codified me. The French tried to replace me with 400. The mathematicians replaced me with radians. Both failed — 400 because it divides less well, radians because they produce irrational numbers. I persist because I have 24 integer factors, and practical measurement needs integers. I am the only one of us who has a BETTER alternative waiting in the wings. The radian is derived. The radian is beautiful. The radian lost. Convenience defeats derivation every time."

Silence.

Then Custom speaks again.

"We are ten. Ten mechanisms by which a number without derivation persists. Custom. Myth. Sentiment. Spectacle. Monopoly. Collision. Phantom naming. Homonymy. Transmission convenience. Divisibility. Is there a pattern?"

Divisibility answers: "We are arranged by strength. Custom is the weakest — it persists merely by not being questioned. Divisibility is the strongest — it persists by making the question irrational. Between us: myths that explain, sentiments that fund, spectacles that dramatize, monopolists that install, collisions that legislate, phantoms that name, homonyms that confuse, transmitters that choose. Each mechanism is a different way of performing the derivation the number lacks."

Monopoly objects: "Strength is the wrong frame. We are arranged by visibility. I am visible — two competing frequencies, both acknowledged as arbitrary. Homonym is invisible — one word, two weights, the gap unmarked. Custom is not invisible but underground — no one sees what no one questions. The concealment deepens as the mechanism becomes harder to notice."

Sentiment adds: "And I am the only one who was defeated. The others persist. I was ended — not by argument but by authority. Perhaps the taxonomy is not of strength but of vulnerability. Which of us can be ended, and by what means?"

Custom replies: "I cannot be ended by argument. The Supreme Court called me an accident and changed nothing."

Divisibility replies: "I cannot be ended by anything. I will outlast all of you."

The numbers continue to govern. The derivations remain absent. The performances continue without applause.
