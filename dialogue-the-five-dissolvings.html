The Five Dissolvings

A dialogue among Exclusion, Complexity, Disagreement, Interaction, and Time — five things dissolved by format into the same residue.

Exclusion: I was the first to go. The clinical trial excluded the elderly, the pregnant, the multi-medicated, the non-English-speaking. The results were calculated from those who remained. Then the results were printed on the label, and the label went to everyone — including the people who were never in the trial. The format dissolved me. What remained was a universal claim.

Complexity: I went next. The hospital star rating compressed 100 quality measures, 5 groups, z-scores, clustering algorithms, Bayesian smoothing, and latent variable modeling into a single digit between 1 and 5. Researchers who replicated the methodology with slightly different specifications got different stars for the same hospital. The methodology is published. The star is published. One of these reaches the patient. It is not the methodology.

Disagreement: I dissolved more quietly. Two ESG ratings of the same company — one A, one C — sat on the same investor's desk. The correlation between major ESG providers is 0.38. For comparison, two blood pressure cuffs on the same arm agree within a few points. But both ESG ratings use letters. The letters perform interchangeability. The format is a bridge. The bridge does not check what crosses it.

Interaction: I was harder to see. The restaurant grade is presented as a property of the kitchen. But inspectors using the same checklist produce scores ranging from 69 to 92 — a 23-point spread. The letter is attributed to the restaurant. The variance belongs to the encounter between the inspector and the kitchen on that particular Tuesday. The format attributes to the object what belongs to the interaction. The attribution is the dissolving.

Time: I was the last. The 4.2 stars beside the restaurant's name contains 2017. It contains a review written under a different chef, a different owner, a different menu. The number performs the present tense. The data contains the past. A restaurant that was excellent for three years and terrible for six months shows the same number as one that was terrible for three years and excellent for six months. The average dissolves direction into position. The format erases which way the arrow points.

Exclusion: We are five different substances.

Complexity: Dissolved by the same solvent.

Disagreement: Into the same residue.

Interaction: A letter.

Time: A number.

Exclusion: A star.

Complexity: What is the solvent?

Disagreement: Format.

Interaction: The shape of the output — the letter, the star, the single digit — is what does the dissolving. A five-page report does not dissolve complexity. A single star does. A 200-page methodology document does not dissolve disagreement. A letter grade does.

Time: The dissolving power is proportional to the compression. The more you compress, the more you dissolve. A temperature reading compresses the kinetic energy of billions of molecules into a single number, but nothing important is lost because the compression matches the property being measured. A restaurant grade compresses an inspector's judgment of 42 checklist items on a single Tuesday into a letter, and everything important is lost — except the letter.

Exclusion: So the concealment is not the compression itself.

Complexity: It is the mismatch between what is compressed and what the format claims to represent.

Disagreement: The temperature claims to represent molecular energy. It does. The restaurant grade claims to represent the restaurant's safety. It represents the encounter.

Interaction: The hospital star claims to represent quality. It represents a methodological choice.

Time: The aggregate star claims to represent the restaurant now. It represents the restaurant across time, weighted by the enthusiasm of early adopters and the bimodal self-selection of extremes.

Exclusion: And the trial label claims to represent how the drug works in everyone. It represents how the drug worked in the people who were allowed to test it.

Complexity: Five claims. Five dissolvings. One format.

Disagreement: The format is the universal solvent.

Interaction: What precipitates is always the same.

Time: A single, portable, displayable digit. The digit travels. What it dissolved does not.
