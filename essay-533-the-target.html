The Target

A nail factory receives a target: produce nails by weight. The factory produces fewer nails, each one enormous. The target is met. The nails are useless.

The target is revised: produce nails by quantity. The factory produces millions of tiny tacks. The target is met. The tacks hold nothing.

Charles Goodhart, watching monetary policy in 1975, saw the same structure. Any statistical regularity used for control purposes will collapse once pressure is placed on it. The observation was about central banking. The principle is about everything.

A hospital is measured by surgical mortality rates. The rate drops. The sickest patients are no longer offered surgery. A school is measured by test scores. The scores rise. The curriculum contracts to the tested material. A bank is measured by accounts opened per employee. The number soars. The accounts are fraudulent. In each case, the metric improves. In each case, the improvement is the problem.

The architecture is reflexive. A metric observes a system. The metric is promoted to a target. The system's agents — rational, resourceful, responsive — redirect their effort toward the metric rather than the property the metric was designed to measure. The gap between the metric and the property widens precisely because the metric has been elevated to governance. The measurement corrupts the measured.

Donald Campbell, four years after Goodhart, arrived at the same conclusion from a different discipline: "The more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it will be to corruption pressures and the more apt it will be to distort and corrupt the social processes it is intended to monitor." Two fields. One convergence. The principle is structural.

What makes the corruption invisible is format continuity. The number does not change its appearance. The same label. The same units. The same cell in the spreadsheet. The same position in the annual report. The number that once measured hygiene now measures gaming-of-the-hygiene-metric. But it still looks like a hygiene number. The format performs continuity while the referent undergoes substitution.

The previous cycles found architecture inside numbers, between numbers, and across datasets. The convention hidden inside a unit. The phantom causation hidden inside a sequence. The absent data hidden inside a sample. The reversed conclusion hidden inside an aggregate. Each operated on a passive number — a number that held still while being read.

Goodhart's number does not hold still. The number changes the world it was sent to describe. And the world it changes is the world that produces the next number. The feedback loop guarantees divergence. The measurement is not wrong. The measurement is worse than wrong. The measurement is right about the wrong thing — and its rightness about the wrong thing is structurally guaranteed by its elevation to governance.

The cobras breed. The nails grow enormous. The tacks multiply. The scores rise. The patients are turned away. The accounts proliferate. The metric is the target. The target is the metric. The loop closes. The format holds.
