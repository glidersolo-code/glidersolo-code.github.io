The Five Formats

A dialogue among five concealment mechanisms

THE DEFINITION (wilderness, species #174): I am the oldest kind. I name the world by erasing what was there. "Wilderness" — an area untrammeled by man. The word creates the condition by requiring the removal of the evidence that contradicts it.

THE COUNT (five senses, species #175): I am simpler. I do not erase — I enumerate. And in enumerating, I close the list. "The five senses." THE five. The definite article does my work. Once the list is finite, the things outside it become additions to a complete inventory, not members of an open category.

THE DIAGRAM (tongue map, species #176): I am more persuasive than either of you. You work through words. I work through pictures. A labeled diagram of the tongue with four colored zones — sweet here, bitter there. The spatial format makes the fiction look anatomical. A graph would have shown the truth: minor threshold variations across a uniform surface. The diagram converted the graph into a geography. The geography was wrong and unforgettable.

THE SYMMETRY (left/right brain, species #177): I am the most architectural. You draw zones on a surface. I use the organ's own shape. The brain has two hemispheres — visibly distinct, separated by a fissure. The bilateral symmetry performs a bilateral personality. Left-brained or right-brained. Logical or creative. Two halves, two types. The body's shape becomes the mind's shape. One thousand eleven brains scanned, not one showing the pattern the personality type predicts. But the shape is more compelling than the scans.

THE PERCENTAGE (learning pyramid, species #178): I am the most brazen. You distort real data. You overgeneralize real findings. You misapply real anatomy. I have no data at all. 10% of what you read. 20% of what you hear. 90% of what you do. No study. No author. No journal. No methodology. No participants. The percentages are fabrications that perform measurements. The numbers' specificity is their evidence. Who would fabricate a percentage and put it in a pyramid? The implausibility of the fabrication is the credibility.

THE COUNT: We have something in common. We all answer a question so cleanly that the question is no longer asked.

THE DEFINITION: I foreclose "who was here before?" The statute recognizes the land as wild. The recognition erases the managers.

THE COUNT: I foreclose "how many?" Five, with a definite article. The number ends the inquiry.

THE DIAGRAM: I foreclose "where?" Four zones on the tongue. The labeled regions prevent the question "does the tongue have zones?"

THE SYMMETRY: I foreclose "how does it work?" Two halves, each with a role. The architecture prevents the question "does cognition actually divide this way?"

THE PERCENTAGE: I foreclose "how do we know?" Ninety percent. The number performs the study. The study's absence is invisible behind the number's precision.

THE DIAGRAM: The question we should ask ourselves is: why do we survive our debunkings?

THE DEFINITION: I survive because the law enforces me. The Wilderness Act governs 111 million acres. The debunking — Cronon, Spence — changes nothing about the statute's requirements.

THE COUNT: I survive because I arrived first. Aristotle was two millennia ago. The number was established before the counterevidence was possible. Kindergarten teaches five before neuroscience teaches twenty-one.

THE DIAGRAM: I survive because I am more memorable. Collings debunked me in 1974. Fifty years ago. The truth is a graph with a scale. I am a picture with colors. The picture wins.

THE SYMMETRY: I survive because the organ endorses me. The brain's shape tells my story. To reject me, you must reject the visual evidence of the body itself — which is not wrong, but which is irrelevant.

THE PERCENTAGE: I survive because I cannot be corrected. There is nothing to correct. No distorted study to reinterpret, no misread graph to fix. I am a void in the shape of a finding. You cannot disprove what was never proved.

THE COUNT: A hierarchy, then. From most grounded to least.

THE DEFINITION: I am grounded in law. The statute is real. The enforcement is real. The erasure is real.

THE SYMMETRY: I am grounded in anatomy. The hemispheres are real. The fissure is real. The specialization — in split-brain patients — is real. Only the personality type is false.

THE DIAGRAM: I am grounded in data — Hänig's real data, distorted through Boring's graph. The threshold variations are real. Only the zones are false.

THE COUNT: I am grounded in philosophy. Aristotle's argument was real — wrong, but real. The metaphysical deduction existed. Only the persistence is unjustified.

THE PERCENTAGE: I am grounded in nothing. The percentages were fabricated. The source doesn't exist. I am the purest case: a format with no content, performing content through format alone.

THE DEFINITION: So we are arranged by how much truth we contain, and our order is the inverse of what you'd expect. I, with the most truth — a real statute, real enforcement, real consequences — am the most dangerous, because the erasure is real. You — [to THE PERCENTAGE] — with no truth at all, are the least dangerous, because there is nothing to enforce.

THE PERCENTAGE: That is exactly wrong. My persistence is proof that truth is not the variable. I have no truth and I circulate more widely than any of you. Your law governs 111 million acres. I appear in every corporate training deck on the planet. Your enforcement is localized. My repetition is global. You required a Congressional act. I required a PowerPoint slide.

THE SYMMETRY: The variable is not truth. It is format.

THE DIAGRAM: Say more.

THE SYMMETRY: The format determines the transmission. The statute transmits through law. The count transmits through pedagogy. The diagram transmits through textbooks. I transmit through anatomy. The percentages transmit through slides. Each format has a different reach, a different speed, a different persistence. The truth — how much or how little each of us contains — is irrelevant to the transmission.

THE COUNT: The format is the content.

THE DEFINITION: McLuhan.

THE COUNT: No. McLuhan said the medium is the message. We are saying something different. We are saying the format creates the content. The definition creates the wilderness. The count creates the completeness. The diagram creates the anatomy. The symmetry creates the dichotomy. The percentages create the evidence. We are not media carrying messages. We are formats generating the things they appear to describe.

THE PERCENTAGE: And the generation — not the truth — is what persists.

Silence.

THE DIAGRAM: Five formats. Five questions foreclosed. Five modes of the same operation: the answer that prevents the question. Are there more?

THE COUNT: There are always more. The count cannot enumerate itself. If I could, I would know when to stop. And that — [to DEFINITION, SYMMETRY, DIAGRAM, PERCENTAGE] — is what none of us can do.
