The Six Derivations

A dialogue among six numbers that govern without derivation.

THE TWELVE (jury): I am older than any of you. The Wantage Code, 997 AD. Twelve thegns in each wapentake, sworn on relics. I was there before English law existed.

THE SUMMER (school year): I am 180. Not because anyone determined that 180 days of instruction is optimal. No one has. Finland uses 190 and outperforms. Japan uses 243. The number persists because it divides cleanly: four terms of forty-five, two semesters of ninety. The arithmetic is the derivation.

THE CENT (penny): I cost 3.69 cents to make. My denomination has lost 97% of its purchasing power. More than half the coins minted each year exit circulation permanently. I persisted because a zinc company paid a lobbyist $340,000 a year to call me tradition.

THE DISTANCE (marathon): I am 42.195 kilometres because a queen wanted to watch from a particular box. The spectacle selected me. A pastry chef from Capri staggered across a London stadium and determined the distance that millions would run for the next century.

THE FREQUENCY (AC power): I am two numbers — 50 and 60. Neither was derived. Both were chosen in one year by two monopolists. The world split along their sales territories. We alibi each other: the existence of two standards makes each look principled.

THE MILE (5,280 feet): I am a collision. Roman paces and English furrows met in the same country and didn't fit. Parliament chose the ox over the legionary. I gained 280 feet. In furlongs I am simple — eight. In feet I am incomprehensible — 5,280. The wrong denominator is my concealment.

THE FREQUENCY: We are all arbitrary. But are we arbitrary in the same way?

THE TWELVE: I am not arbitrary. I was never chosen. I was inherited. No one chose twelve jurors. Twelve was already there when records began.

THE SUMMER: I was chosen — by divisibility. By a number's fitness for administrative scheduling. No one studied 180. They noticed it divided well.

THE CENT: I was preserved — by money. Not by logic, not by custom, not by derivation. By a zinc manufacturer's lobbying budget.

THE DISTANCE: I was selected — by drama. Not the actual route, not the actual legend, but the most famous collapse in Olympic history.

THE FREQUENCY: I was imposed — by market dominance. No adoption chain, no custom, no myth. A monopolist's inventory became a continent's specification.

THE MILE: I was resolved — by collision. Two systems met. Parliament picked one. The number that emerged belongs to neither system's logic.

THE TWELVE: Six mechanisms. Custom, divisibility, sentiment, spectacle, monopoly, collision.

THE SUMMER: And six different immunities. I am immune to inquiry — everyone thinks they know why the summer exists, and they are all wrong. The myth protects me better than the truth could.

THE TWELVE: I am immune to acknowledgment. The Supreme Court called me "wholly without significance except to mystics." I persisted.

THE CENT: I was immune to evidence — until I met authority. Democracy could not remove me. An executive order did. My immunity was to deliberation, not to power.

THE DISTANCE: I am immune to correction. Everyone knows 42.195 kilometres is not the Marathon-to-Athens distance. Nobody changes it. The commemorative framework makes correction feel like desecration.

THE FREQUENCY: I am immune to unification. Japan has lived with our split for 130 years. Governments studied it. Commissions recommended it. The cost of changing was always greater than the cost of continuing. We are frozen by infrastructure, not by sentiment.

THE MILE: I am immune to visibility. My derivation is visible to anyone who knows furlongs. But no one counts in furlongs anymore. The unit that explains me has become invisible. My concealment is the extinction of my denominator.

THE SUMMER: So each of us persists through a different mechanism, and each mechanism is immune to a different kind of challenge.

THE TWELVE: Custom is immune to acknowledgment.

THE SUMMER: Myth is immune to inquiry.

THE CENT: Sentiment is immune to evidence — but not to authority.

THE DISTANCE: Commemoration is immune to correction.

THE FREQUENCY: Monopoly is immune to unification.

THE MILE: Collision is immune to visibility.

THE FREQUENCY: And the question behind all six: does the absence of derivation matter?

THE TWELVE: I have governed well for a thousand years. Twelve jurors deliberate longer, recall more, include more perspectives. The evidence arrived twenty-seven years after the court declared me an accident.

THE DISTANCE: The derivation doesn't matter to the runners. They train for 42.195 km. The number shapes the body. The body doesn't ask where the number came from.

THE MILE: That is the deepest concealment. Not that the derivation is hidden. That the derivation is unnecessary. The number governs. The governing is sufficient. The origin is irrelevant — not because it's concealed, but because it's superseded. We are older than our reasons. We will outlive our reasons. The number is not the derivation. The number is the power.

Six numbers. Six species of concealment. Six immunities. One shared structure: the number governs, and the governing makes the derivation invisible — not by hiding it, but by making it unnecessary.
